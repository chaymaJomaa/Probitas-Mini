Un collage de citations

Les auteurs de CAPE constatent que « the CAM explanation only offers relative attention information, that is, on an attention heatmap, we can interpret which image region is more or less important than the others » (Chowdhury et al., 2024, p. 1). Ils ajoutent que « these regions cannot be meaningfully compared across classes, and the contribution of each region to the model's class prediction is not revealed » (Chowdhury et al., 2024, p. 1). De leur côté, Zhang et ses collègues observent que « CAM often struggles to identify discriminative regions that distinguish visually similar fine-grained classes » (Zhang et al., 2025, p. 9611).

Toutes ces citations sont correctes, mais le devoir ne contient presque rien d'autre.
